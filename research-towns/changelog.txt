---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-10-07
  Features:
    - Town halls replace labs: research happens in the towns of the people of Nauvis, supplied with science packs, goods, fluids and power through depots.
    - Towns are placed by the map generator (Towns slider, visible in the map preview); the first town near the landing site is your partner right away.
    - Other towns are discovered by walking up to them; deliver a gift through a depot to make them your partners.
    - Each town level opens one science pack; infinite levels beyond the last science add research productivity.
    - Houses speed up research and are upgraded with surplus deliveries; total speed bonus capped at +120 %.
    - Towns continuously use the materials of completed milestones (whole items per minute, a 5 minute stock).
    - Town specializations: each town is skilled in one basic material derived from the game data; from town level 6 a partner town raises the productivity of all recipes making it (1 % per level above 5, at most 15 % per town and 25 % per material, diminishing returns for repeats).
    - Researching town halls and level 5 houses absorb pollution.
    - A town hall destroyed by the biters leaves ruins; the town survives and is rebuilt at the same level with materials delivered through its depots.
    - Town substation: a real substation connected to your grid with a wire; the town takes power only through it.
    - Town board sends the town's requests and progress to the circuit network.
    - Town hall panel with milestone, house upgrade, upkeep and specialization; progress bars.
    - Mod settings: material upkeep multiplier, pollution absorption multiplier (startup) and town specialization strength (map setting, can be changed in a running game).
    - Story: the people of Nauvis as research partners – flavour texts, town messages and Tips and tricks.
  Graphics:
    - Town halls in five eras (settlement, automation, logistics, chemistry, city of science), rebuilt with your research; houses by house level; ruins of each era.
    - Machines, trees, ground textures and items from the game in the town renders.
    - Depots: a 2×2 goods warehouse with goods on pallets, a fluid tank with an operator hut, the town substation and a town board.
    - Town halls and houses light up their surroundings at night.
    - A researching town hall smokes from its chimneys, so you can see it working by day.
    - Town buildings are linked by garlands of flags and lanterns, later by wooden and paved walkways; hover a building to see the paths of its town network.
  Compatibility:
    - Factorio 2.0 base game and Space Age.
    - Tested with Bob's mods and Pyanodon: sciences, milestone materials, labs and specializations are derived automatically.
